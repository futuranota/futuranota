## Calendar

Connect {brand}'s launch date with Calendar. The 7d, 15d, and 31d frames search important events forward from your anchor date. That reveals a much wider opportunity scope than launch day alone, regardless of what product you plan to ship.

Action: sync launch date → Intel → Calendar → try 15d first.

## Forecast

Forecast covers only the next 5 days from today. It does not replace Calendar for long-range planning. Use it when {brand}'s event is close: last-minute content, logistics, or weather-driven opportunities.

Action: use it the week before the event, not months ahead.

## Market Hype

This panel shows where {brand} concentrates attention now: TikTok, YouTube, press, X. That index shows where their hottest customers spend time and where you should invest attention once you have content. When your product exists: pick channels, create content, iterate, and boost the winning post.

Action: Intel → Market Hype → study channel mix.

## Search Intent

Search trends show what {brand}'s audience looks for on Google. They guide what to write, talk about, promote, and which pain points resonate.

Action: use top keywords from the panel as headlines or research questions.

## YouTube

Paste YouTube or Vimeo links in Intel → YouTube. Watch in-app and take visual notes. This is your learning space inside Futura Nota.

Action: one official or coverage video about {brand} → timestamped notes.
